RESEARCH · COMPUTER VISION2026

Vision-based powder flow optimization

Built a computer vision pipeline that screens powder-feed settings for directed energy deposition from video, extracting 15 features across 36 experiments using mass-weighted edge detection, optical flow and FFT analysis.

Ranked conditions with six MCDM methods (PCA-TOPSIS, AHP, entropy weighting, MOORA). The methods agreed at Spearman ρ ≥ 0.81, and the top condition stayed in the top 5 across 500 weight perturbations. The best conditions were confirmed through DED printing; manuscript in preparation.

  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • Scikit-learn
  • Statsmodels

RUL PREDICTION13.1

MACHINE LEARNINGNASA CMAPSS

Aircraft engine predictive maintenance

Built a leakage-free RUL pipeline on NASA C-MAPSS with LSTM and Transformer models, reaching RMSE 13.4 and 13.1 on FD001, a 68% reduction over a mean predictor. Results are averaged over 3 seeds and benchmarked against Ridge and Random Forest.

Showed through ablation that positional encoding cuts Transformer RMSE from 15.9 to 12.9, and trained an autoencoder on healthy engines for anomaly detection (AUROC 0.76).

  • PyTorch
  • Scikit-learn
  • NumPy
